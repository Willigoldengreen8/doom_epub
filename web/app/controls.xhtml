CHAPTER II

The Controls

W SWalk forward and back
A DTurn left and right
Q EStep (strafe) left and right
F or a tapFire
RUse — open doors, press switches, take lifts
EnterChoose in menus
EscOpen the game menu
TabAutomap
1 – 7Select a weapon
F2 F3Save and load games

Click or tap the game plate once before using the keyboard: the page needs focus. Apple Books keeps the arrow keys and Space for page-turning, and macOS turns Ctrl into dictation, so the letters above are the controls to use there. On touch screens, the engraved buttons beneath the plate stand in for the keyboard, and dragging across the plate turns the player.
