CHAPTER IV

A Note on This Edition

This edition was set in IM Fell English, Didot, Baskerville and Iowan Old Style. The cover is original lettering; the frontispiece plate is an engraving in the manner of the old masters.

The engine is DoomGeneric, a portable implementation of DOOM, compiled to WebAssembly with Emscripten and given a small platform layer for this book. The soundtrack is rendered at play time by the OPL2 music player from Chocolate Doom together with the Nuked OPL3 emulator, using the instruments stored in the IWAD — no additional sound data is packed into the file. Sound effects go through SDL2’s WebAudio port.

The EPUB was assembled from source by packaging/build_epub.py; the build is byte-for-byte reproducible, and every release candidate is validated with epubcheck before it is published. The publisher’s device on the title page is set from typographic rules, as devices were, before anyone thought to draw one.

This edition was prepared by Artem Kulyk.

May the file open in your reader of choice, and may the imps be where you remember them.
