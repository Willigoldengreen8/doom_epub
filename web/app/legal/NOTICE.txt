doom.epub - a real ebook that runs real DOOM
============================================

ENGINE
------
DoomGeneric - https://github.com/ozkl/doomgeneric
Copyright (C) 2020-2024 Ozkan Sezer and contributors.
Licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 2 (see GPL-2.0.txt).

The WebAssembly build inside this EPUB is a compiled and minimally patched
version of DoomGeneric. The corresponding source code, Emscripten build
scripts, the two engine patches and full build instructions are published at

    https://github.com/artemkulyk/doom_epub

along with the tagged release this EPUB was built from. Per the GNU GPL, that
source is the preferred form for modification and is available from the same
place this EPUB was obtained.

MUSIC (OPL2 SYNTHESIS)
----------------------
Music playback uses the OPL2/OPL3 music player from Chocolate Doom
(https://www.chocolate-doom.org/), Copyright (C) 1993-1996 id Software, Inc.,
Copyright (C) 2005-2014 Simon Howard, and the Nuked OPL3 emulator by
Alexey Khokholov (Nuke.YKT). Both are licensed under the GNU General Public
License, version 2 or later. These files are vendored in the source
repository under web/engine/opl/ with their original copyright headers;
web/engine/opl/epub_opl.c is a single-threaded adaptation of Chocolate Doom's
SDL backend for the EPUB environment. No additional instrument data is
required: the GENMIDI instruments used by the synthesizer come from the
shareware IWAD already present in this EPUB.

declared with the manifest property "scripted". Every runtime asset is inside
the EPUB container; no network access is required or performed.
