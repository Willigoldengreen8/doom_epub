CHAPTER I

Before You Begin

This is a real ebook. It is a single, standards-valid EPUB 3 file: no network access, no plugins, no external files, no modified reader. It is also the complete 1993 shareware episode of DOOM — the actual engine, compiled to WebAssembly, drawing into a framebuffer and ticking at its original thirty-five frames per second, entirely inside your ebook reader.

The edition began as a question. What do ordinary, unmodified readers actually allow? The answer surprised us. Scripted, fixed-layout EPUB 3, a capability declared by the standard itself, is a real runtime: canvas framebuffers, WebAssembly, keyboard and pointer input, packaged binary assets, local storage and audio all work inside it. That is enough for a game.

Inside are the portable DOOM engine and the official shareware IWAD: nine levels, sound effects, OPL2 music and saved games. Everything the book needs is already here, inside the file you are holding.
